Security

Security first, by design

We're designing GDR around the principle that protecting customer assets comes before everything else. Our planned approach includes:

  • Account protection — two-factor authentication and withdrawal safeguards.
  • Asset segregation — keeping customer funds separate from operating funds.
  • Cold storage — holding the majority of assets offline.
  • Independent review — external security testing before launch.
